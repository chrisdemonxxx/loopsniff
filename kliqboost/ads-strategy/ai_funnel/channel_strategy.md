## Content Dos and Donts

### DO
- Post consistently (3-5x/week minimum)
- Use relevant emojis (not excessive)
- Include a CTA in every post (soft or hard)
- Share real numbers and results
- Respond to comments in the discussion group
- Use polls and questions for engagement
- Keep posts under 300 words

### DO NOT
- Post walls of text
- Use clickbait titles
- Post more than 2x/day (channel fatigue)
- Ignore comments or questions
- Copy competitors content verbatim
- Post only sales content (80% value, 20% sales)
- Use ALL CAPS (except sparingly for emphasis)
