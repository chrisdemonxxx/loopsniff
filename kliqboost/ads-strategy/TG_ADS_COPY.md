## A/B Testing Plan

### Week 1: Test ad copy ($5/day per variant)
- Submit TEST AD first — wait for approval
- Once approved, run Ad 1A vs 3A vs 3B
- Measure: click rate, message rate, cost per message

### Week 2: Scale winner ($10-20/day)
- Take best performer
- Expand targeting to more channels
- Test Russian versions

### Week 3+: Optimize ($20-50/day)
- Kill underperformers (CTR < 0.5%)
- Scale winners
- Test new variations of top performers

## Key Metrics to Track
- **CPM**: Cost per 1000 impressions (target: 0.5-2 TON)
- **CTR**: Click-through rate (target: >1%)
- **CPL**: Cost per lead/message (target: <$5)
- **Conversion**: Messages → Paying clients (target: >10%)
