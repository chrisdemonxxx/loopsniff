# Phase 1: Discovery Budget Plan (Weeks 1-2)

## Objective: Find winning channels + copy combinations
## Total Budget: 150-300 TON (~$525-$1,050 at $3.50/TON)
## Daily Budget: 10-20 TON/day (~$35-$70/day)
## Duration: 14 days

---

## Budget Allocation by Ad Set

| Ad Set | % Budget | Daily TON | Total TON (14 days) | Purpose |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------------------|---------|
| AS-01: Core Media Buyers | 40% | 6.0 | 84 | Highest priority — find winning copy |
| AS-02: Affiliate/CPA | 25% | 3.75 | 52.5 | Secondary volume |
| AS-03: Gambling/Betting | 15% | 2.25 | 31.5 | Niche testing |
| AS-05: Platform Specific | 10% | 1.5 | 21 | Platform angle test |
| Testing Reserve | 10% | 1.5 | 21 | New channel discovery |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **15 TON** | **210 TON** | |

## Ads to Launch

### Week 1 — Launch 6 ads across 3 ad sets:

| # | Ad Set | Copy Type | ID | CPM Bid |
|---|--------|-----------|-----|---------|
| 1 | Core MB | Pain Point PP-01 | DISC-01 | 0.5 TON |
| 2 | Core MB | Benefit BF-01 | DISC-02 | 0.5 TON |
| 3 | Core MB | Question QH-01 | DISC-03 | 0.5 TON |
| 4 | Affiliate | Fresh Accounts PP-02 | DISC-04 | 0.4 TON |
| 5 | Affiliate | CPA Focus RU-06 | DISC-05 | 0.4 TON |
| 6 | Gambling | Compliant RU-07 | DISC-06 | 0.5 TON |

### Week 2 — Based on Week 1 results:
- Kill bottom 50% performers (lowest CTR)
- Launch 3-4 new challengers based on winning angles
- Increase budget on winning ad set by 50%

## Daily Monitoring Checklist

- [ ] Check CTR for each ad (target: >0.3%)
- [ ] Check impression delivery (are bids high enough?)
- [ ] Check channel-level performance (which channels deliver?)
- [ ] Monitor TON/USD exchange rate
- [ ] Track bot interactions / channel joins
- [ ] Note any ads rejected by moderation

## Key Decisions at End of Phase 1

1. **Which copy angle wins?** (Pain vs Benefit vs Question)
2. **Which channels deliver?** (Remove non-performers)
3. **What's the optimal CPM bid?** (Start low, increase)
4. **Image or text-only?** (Launch AB-07 if budget allows)
5. **Channel vs Bot destination?** (Which converts better)

## Phase 1 Exit Criteria
✅ At least 2 ads with CTR > 0.3%
✅ At least 1 ad set with cost per join < $3
✅ At least 10 bot interactions or channel joins
✅ Identified top 5 performing channels
