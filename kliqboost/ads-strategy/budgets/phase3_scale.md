# Phase 3: Scale Budget Plan (Month 2+)

## Objective: Maximize qualified lead volume at target CPA
## Total Budget: 600-1,500+ TON/month (~$2,100-$5,250+/month)
## Daily Budget: 20-50+ TON/day (~$70-$175+/day)
## Duration: Ongoing

---

## Budget Allocation (Performance-Driven)

| Ad Set | % Budget | Monthly TON | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Top Performer #1 | 35% | 350 | Highest ROI ad set |
| Top Performer #2 | 25% | 250 | Second-best ad set |
| Expansion: New verticals | 15% | 150 | Test new channel niches |
| Direct Channel Deals | 15% | 150 | Negotiate native posts |
| Continuous Testing | 10% | 100 | Always-on A/B testing |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **1,000 TON** | |

## Scaling Rules

### When to Increase Budget
- CTR stable at >0.4% for 7+ days
- Cost per qualified lead < target ($50)
- Bot qualification rate > 15%
- Client conversion rate > 5%

### When to Pause/Reduce
- CTR drops below 0.2% (creative fatigue)
- Cost per lead increases >30% week-over-week
- No qualified leads for 5+ consecutive days
- Channel saturation (same users seeing ads too often)

## Creative Refresh Schedule
| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| Week 1-2 | Run current winners |
| Week 3 | Introduce 2 new challengers |
| Week 4 | Kill lowest performer, add 1 new |
| Monthly | Complete copy refresh (new angles) |

## Expansion Opportunities
1. **New languages**: Test Arabic, Spanish, Hindi copies
2. **Search targeting**: Test keyword targeting (Search mode)
3. **Bot targeting**: Target users of competitor bots
4. **Direct deals**: 5-10 channel partnerships at $100-$300/post
5. **Mini App ads**: If RichAds/ad networks support, test Mini App format
6. **Cross-platform**: Export TG leads to Meta/Google for retargeting
