## How TG Ads Targeting Works

Telegram does **not** have a traditional keyword/interest system like Meta or Google. Targeting works via:

1. **Channel topic selection** — pick broad categories (Business, Marketing, Crypto, etc.)
2. **Manual channel whitelisting** — target up to 100 specific channels by @username
3. **Search interest keywords** — used to find/filter channels for placement

**Tools for channel discovery:** TGStat, Telemetrio, Feedspy
