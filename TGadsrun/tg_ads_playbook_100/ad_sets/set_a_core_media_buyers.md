## Metrics & Kill/Scale Rules

| Metric | Target | Kill if... | Scale if... |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| CTR | >0.3% | <0.15% after 3 days | >0.5% consistently |
| Cost per click | <$1.00 | >$3.00 | <$0.50 |
| Bot DMs | >3 in 5 days | 0 after $15 spent | >1/day |
| Impressions/day | >1,000 | <200 (raise CPM) | >3,000 |

## Day 5 Decision

- **Winner found (CTR >0.3%)**: Clone into Ad Set B, double budget
- **No clear winner**: Swap worst performer for the winner's challenger copy (e.g., NICHE-BSOD-02-RU)
- **All failing (<0.15%)**: Try NICHE-AGENCY-01-RU (premium brand angle) or raise CPM to 0.8 TON
- **Multiple winners**: Great! Keep all winners running, kill the worst one, add niche-specific challenger
