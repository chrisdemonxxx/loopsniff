## Test 6: Frequency Cap — 1x vs 2x/day
**Budget needed**: $80+ ($40 per variant)
**When**: After 14+ days baseline data

| Variant | Freq Cap | Hypothesis |
|---------|----------|------------|
| A: 1x/day | Show ad once per user per 24h | Less annoying, but fewer impressions |
| B: 2x/day | Show ad twice per user per 24h | More touches = more conversions, or more ad fatigue? |

**Why this matters**: At $100 budget, 1x is safer (wider reach). At scale, 2x might increase conversion by reinforcing the message. But it could also annoy users and hurt CTR.
